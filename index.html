MeteoVelo

How much does the weather change bike traffic in Lyon?

Code & report ↗
0.95R² on a week the model never saw
1,350bikes/h mean absolute error
−33 %RMSE vs. same hour last week

I merged 263,000 hourly counts from 376 bike counters with Lyon's open temperature and rain sensors in PySpark, then compared four Spark MLlib models. Gradient-boosted trees won.

A random split on hourly data lets a model peek at the days it is tested on, so I re-validated the result on a strictly later week, against a naive baseline. It held. The figure is that week, hour by hour.

Duo

A private drawing app for two people.

Code ↗
9klines of Dart
64tests (39 unit, 25 security rules)
v1.4released on Android

One person draws and taps Send. The other sees it replayed stroke by stroke a few seconds later. Flutter and Firebase, with an offline queue, a home-screen widget, and Firestore security rules that act as the whole backend.

Drawings are stored as vectors. A finger on a 120 Hz screen produces far more points than a stroke needs, and a Firestore document is capped at 1 MiB, so each stroke is simplified with the Ramer–Douglas–Peucker algorithm before upload. Draw in the box to watch it work.

Personal project · designed, built and shipped alone · Sep 2026

RL trading agent

Can a PPO agent beat buy & hold once fees are paid?

Code & report ↗
9agents: 3 assets × 3 seeds
0 / 3assets where it beats buy & hold on unseen 2025 data
0.1 %fee charged on every trade

PPO agents decide every hour whether to be invested or in cash, on hourly BTC, ETH and gold data. Features use the past only, and their scaling is fitted on the training years alone.

The agents beat buy & hold on the validation year used to pick them, then lose to it on 2025, which they never saw. The write-up explains why: selection bias from keeping the best checkpoint, agents that mostly learned to stay invested, and large variance across seeds.
